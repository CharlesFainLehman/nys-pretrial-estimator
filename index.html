How to read these numbers

  • Released counts ROR, non-monetary release, and bail-set-and-posted. Remand and bail-set-but-not-posted count as detained.
  • Rearrest and FTA rates are conditional on release — if the defendant was detained, neither event can occur.
  • Violent felony rearrest is the share of rearrests with a violent-felony top charge, not the share of all released defendants.
  • Cells with fewer than 30 matching arraignments are flagged; those numbers are noisy.
Source: NYS DCJS Supplemental Pretrial Release Data File · Adults (18+) arrested on fingerprinted charges, 2020–2025 · Rates pool all years and mix pre-/post-bail-reform regimes
