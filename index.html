How to read these numbers

  • Released counts ROR, non-monetary release, and bail-set-and-posted. Remand and bail-set-but-not-posted count as detained.
  • Rearrest and FTA rates are conditional on release — if the defendant was detained, neither event can occur.
  • Violent felony rearrest is the share of rearrests with a violent-felony top charge, not the share of all released defendants.
  • Cases disposed at arraignment (plea, dismissal, or time-served resolved in court that day) are excluded because they have no pretrial period to measure rearrest or FTA against. This mildly understates the release rate for charges that frequently end at arraignment (low-level misdemeanors, some DWI).
  • Rearrest outcomes for 2024–2025 arraignments are still maturing: recent cohorts have had less time to accrue rearrests, so pooled rates slightly understate the long-run share.
  • Cells with fewer than 30 matching arraignments are flagged; those numbers are noisy.
